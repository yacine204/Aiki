Chambon-sur-Cisse (French pronunciation: [ʃɑ̃bɔ̃ syʁ sis], literally Chambon on Cisse) is a former commune in the Loir-et-Cher department in central France. On 1 January 2017, it was merged into the commune Valencisse.
