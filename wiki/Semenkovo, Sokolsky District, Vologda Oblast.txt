Semenkovo (Russian: Семенково) is a rural locality (a village) in Arkhangelskoye Rural Settlement, Sokolsky District, Vologda Oblast, Russia. The population was 14 as of 2002.


== Geography ==
Semenkovo is located 20 km northwest of Sokol (the district's administrative centre) by road. Gladkino is the nearest rural locality.
