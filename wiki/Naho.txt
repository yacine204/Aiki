Naho (written: 菜穂, 菜保, 奈穂, 南穂 or 直歩) is a feminine Japanese given name. Notable people with the name include:

Naho Emoto (江本 奈穂; born 1985), Japanese softball player
Naho Hoshiyama (星山 菜穂; born 1980), Japanese gymnast
Naho Miyoshi (三好 南穂; born 1993), Japanese basketball player
Naho Sato (佐藤 南帆; born 2001), Japanese tennis player
Naho Sugiyama (杉山 直歩; born 1978), Japanese mixed martial artist
Naho Terashima (寺島 奈穂; born 1993), Japanese ice hockey player
Naho Toda (戸田 菜穂; born 1974), Japanese actress
Naho Yoshimi (吉見 菜保; born 1972), Japanese ice hockey player
